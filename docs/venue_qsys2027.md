## 2. Fit (judgement)
- Hybrid quantum-RL autoscaler (simulator, parameter-matched classical control, no speed-up claim): plausible under "Quantum Applications and Emerging Technologies" or "Quantum Software and Systems". The page does not show whether simulator-only work is welcome.
- Classical-only LSTM/DDQN + transfer autoscaling: out of scope for QSys; fits CCGrid tracks 3 and 5.

## 3. Chances (judgement, no data)
No acceptance statistics exist. A first-edition, co-located conference with an organiser from the cloud community will probably favour quantum-for-systems work, but the quantum reviewers may ask for real-hardware or noise evidence. Risk: a simulator-only result with no advantage claim may be seen as weak by quantum reviewers, and a thin quantum part would not pass. Do not rely on this until the CFP deadlines appear.

## 4. Dual submission policy
- CCGrid 2027 CFP (https://easychair.org/cfp/ccgrid27), verbatim: "Submissions must present original, unpublished research not under review elsewhere." and "Authors should read and follow the IEEE Submission and Peer Review Policies." Also "All submissions must be double-blind."
- QSys page, verbatim: "Submissions must present original, unpublished research not under review elsewhere." No separate dual-submission text.
- IEEE (https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/become-an-ieee-journal-author/publishing-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/submission-and-peer-review-policies/): "Multiple submissions" = a given article concurrently under active consideration by two or more publications; "It is at the discretion of each IEEE Organization Unit whether or not to allow multiple submissions"; authors must disclose similar submissions and state how the new one differs, and cite the earlier work. Note: this page is for journals; the conference-specific PSPB text was not opened (UNVERIFIED for conferences).
- Conclusion: the same paper to both = not allowed. Two papers with distinct contributions (classical autoscaler to CCGrid; quantum extension to QSys) are consistent with the wording, provided the overlap is small and disclosed/cited without breaking double-blind. How much overlap is tolerated is not defined; ask the PC chairs (Santra, Mao) and CCGrid chairs in writing. The two submission windows may not even overlap because QSys has no deadlines yet.

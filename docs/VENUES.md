### Revised recommendation
1. **CCGrid 2027** (Nov 24 / Dec 1): best fit and timing, 10 pp.
2. **ICPE 2027** (Nov 9 / Nov 16): needs a results freeze about Nov 9.
3. **DSN 2027** (Nov 25 / Dec 2): a strong second-wave option with an 11 pp limit. It fits only if the paper is framed around dependability or robustness of learned autoscaling. Submitting to both CCGrid and DSN would be double submission, so pick one.
4. **SIGMETRICS 2027 winter** (Jan 4 / Jan 11): fallback with extra time, but 20 pp and a high bar.
5. **QSys 2027** once deadlines appear, with QCE 2027 as a quantum-track fallback.
PDP 2027 (Oct 19 / Oct 26) is probably infeasible.
